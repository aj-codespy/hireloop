# HireLoop Competitive Landing-Page Intelligence

**Purpose:** Distill the *best* product-visibility, description, and copy moves from the
top AI-hiring / best-in-class SaaS sites, then map them to HireLoop so we launch
directly competitive on day one. All copy below is **verbatim, extracted live** from the
source sites (captured 2026-07-20). HireLoop's counter-copy is a *draft direction*, not
final wording.

**Design guardrails applied:** no em-dashes in any HireLoop draft copy; one accent (orange);
clarity-first; motion serves comprehension. Keep the existing light palette.

## 3. What HireLoop should steal (and how)

We are **not** a chatbot, not a notetaker, not a scheduler-only tool. We own the
**AI interview + human orchestration → qualified candidate list → webhook handoff**.
Our wedge vs these 8: **show the interview actually happening, and make the boundary
unmistakable.**

| Move | From | HireLoop application |
|------|------|----------------------|
| Bold anti-status-quo thesis + stat band | HireVue | H1 that challenges resume-screening; a 4-stat ROI band (time saved, drop-off cut, cost/interview, quality lift) |
| Show the product acting, not described | Paradox, ElevenLabs | A **live AI-interview widget** in the hero: real voice waveform, a question card, a live transcript line. Interactive, not a static screenshot |
| Live "system is working now" counter | Ramp, Mercor | A hero-side or nav ticker: "1,240 interviews completed today / 312 candidates qualified this week" (real data from our DB) |
| Map each feature to a human "why" | Metaview | Every capability card ends with "So you can..." (e.g. "So recruiters review 5 finalists, not 500 resumes") |
| Clear positioning line vs chatbot noise | GoodTime | "Not a chatbot. Not a proctoring tool. The interview engine that qualifies candidates for your team." |
| Restraint + one real product screenshot | Linear | One strong, real candidate-interview UI frame as the hero asset; no fake div dashboards |
| Named enterprise logos | Paradox, ElevenLabs | Real SVG logo wall (logo-only, no category labels) once we have customers |
| Audit trail / guardrails as trust | Metaview, GoodTime | A "Fairness & control" block: audit trail of scoring, proctoring transparency, human-in-the-loop override |

## 5. Anti-patterns to avoid (from the same sites)
- Don't lead with a version label or "AI-powered" as the whole pitch (several do; it's generic).
- Don't fake a product UI out of divs (HireVue's tile grid is fine because it links to real tours; a fake dashboard is a tell).
- Don't stack 6 identical feature cards (use the "why" framing + varied layout).
- Don't use em-dashes anywhere in our copy.
- One CTA intent per action ("Start free" everywhere, not "Start free" + "Get started" + "Sign up free").
